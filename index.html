The weekly rhythm

Monday–Friday, one topic builds toward one deliverable. Saturday is not a new-content day. Sunday is not a screen day.

📘
Topic + Build
Monday – Friday, as designed
🧾
Admin Only
Saturday — no new content, no build quota. Rate your energy 1–10.
🌿
Reflect + Go Outside
Sunday — one activity outside the house. No screens-as-rest.
Two low-energy Fridays in a row → cut next week's scope before Monday, not after you burn out. Take one unscheduled buffer day a month for whatever life throws at you.
